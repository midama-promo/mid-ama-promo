# Temporary service interruption notice

A compact notice is displayed on the public homepage and every client dashboard. It explains that the website is experiencing a technical issue, previously released catalogue remains available on DSPs, catalogue visibility on the website may be temporarily affected, and apologises for the inconvenience.

The notice is informational only; it does not change release records, external customer data, authentication, or storage. Remove the `ServiceNotice` placements from `src/App.tsx` and `src/components/client/ClientDashboard.tsx` when normal catalogue visibility has been restored.
